Generated by `pnpm economics:rtp` from the contract's binomial weights and
on-chain multiplier calculation. The matrix covers rows 8–32 and all three
risk levels (75 combinations).

- RTP range: 90.03%–90.48% (9003–9048 basis points)
- House-edge range: 9.52%–9.97%
- Probability normalization: each row's integer weights sum to 1,000,000
- Regression tolerance: 89.50%–90.50%; the Hardhat test fails outside it

The small upward drift at higher rows/high risk is caused by integer flooring
of normalized multipliers. `scripts/rtp-report.ts` prints every rows/risk/RTP/
house-edge tuple for review.
